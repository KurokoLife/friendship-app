# Copy review: golden rule + ask (Nov 14)

Checks the app's wording against the main rule in 05: treat people the way you'd want to be treated, and ask when you don't know. Nothing here is decided until the founder says OK; approved lines then move into 05 and get built.

Joining (stage 2), Suggestions (stage 3) and Notifications (3b) are already built (Oct 26 to 28), so approved changes to them are copy updates in the code. Planning (5) and the finishing pass (6) build the rest.

How to read it
- **Change**: fails the rule (gives the other person's reaction as the reason, or invites guessing). New wording proposed.
- **Adjust**: small fix (voice word, or a missed chance to point to asking).
- **Keep**: already fits. Lines marked **model** show the rule well; use them as examples.

What this review can't see: wording that only lives in the code (listed at the end). That needs a code sweep.

## 0. Behind the scenes (for code sessions)

AGENTS.md's "AI philosophy" five-step prompt structure has a step 2 "Perspective shift" whose example guesses the other person's feelings ("Sarah may be wondering what she said wrong."). Under golden rule + ask, Limen never guesses for the person. **Change:** step 2 becomes "How would you want to be treated here?" or "ask them". Its own research note already supports this: "I am someone who shows up for people" works better than "showing up makes the other person feel good."

## 1. Joining (built, stage 2)

Intro screens 1 and 2: **Keep.**

"What's hardest about friendship for you right now?": **Keep the question and options.** Two options need a reply line that follows the rule (shown later, in Your circle or the You tab, never as a lecture):
- "I'm usually the one who reaches out" can turn into keeping score. **Add line:** "Reaching out is how you'd want a friend to treat you. You could also ask them how they like to keep in touch."
- "Not sure people want to hear from me" is a guess about someone else. **Add line:** "If a friend got in touch with you, would you mind? If you're not sure about them, you can ask."

"Who would you call with good news? Who could you ask for a hand?": **Keep (model).** It's already about asking.

"Most adults name one to four close friends. None is more common than people think.": **Keep.** It eases worry without promising anything.

How you connect (6 questions, part of profile setup): **Adjust.** Swap one question for a golden rule question the person answers for themselves, privately:
- "If a new friend had to change plans, how would you want to hear?" (As early as possible / A quick message is fine / A call / Just be honest about why)
- Idea for the founder to decide: at "I need to change it", Limen could quietly show the person their own answer ("You said you'd want to hear as early as possible."). It's their own words, not Limen's, but it could feel like a nudge. Your call.
- Optional extra question, notice only: "When you're not sure what someone meant, what do you usually do?" (Ask them / Guess / Let it go / Depends)

"Before you start" agreements:
- "Honest is kinder than quiet": **Change** to "Be honest, the way you'd want people to be with you."
- "Answer every hello, even with a 'no thanks'": **Change** to "Answer every hi, the way you'd want yours answered. A 'no thanks' is fine."
- "Friendship only" and "Meet somewhere public first": **Keep.**

The liking-gap line ("After meeting someone new, most people think it went less well than the other person does."): **Keep.** It corrects your own worry. Where it appears in chat cards, pair it with asking: "If you're wondering how it went for them, you can ask."
